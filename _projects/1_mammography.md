Undergraduate thesis (2022) at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, supervised by Sumaya Ishrat Moyeen, Asst. Prof., RUET.

- Used the CBIS-DDSM dataset from The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA).
- Built a comprehensive mammography preprocessing pipeline, including pectoral muscle removal.
- Designed a hybrid neural network architecture (EfficientNetV2 + SVM) for cancerous lesion classification.

The manuscript is under review at _SN Computer Science_.
