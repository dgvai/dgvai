I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University, where I build and study mixed-reality systems as a Graduate Research Assistant. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and mixed reality.

I earned my B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) in 2022. As an undergraduate I worked on deep learning for computer vision, image processing and data analysis, including a hybrid EfficientNetV2 + SVM pipeline for classifying cancerous lesions in mammograms.

Before my Ph.D., I spent nearly two years as a software engineer at Sazim Tech, leading projects across full-stack development, data analysis and DevOps. I also maintain a number of open-source projects.
