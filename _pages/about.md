I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant on mixed-reality system development and research in MR/AR/VR. My research interests are **computer vision** and **mixed reality**.

I received my B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering from [Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET)](https://www.ruet.ac.bd/) in 2022. During my undergraduate studies I worked on machine learning and deep learning for computer vision, image processing, and data analysis, including a hybrid EfficientNetV2 + SVM approach to cancerous lesion classification in mammography for my undergraduate thesis.

Before starting my Ph.D., I spent nearly two years as a Software Engineer at Sazim Tech Ltd. in Dhaka, working on full-stack development, data analysis, DevOps, and CI/CD, leading projects and mentoring interns. I also maintain several open-source projects, from Laravel packages to a GNOME Shell extension, on [GitHub](https://github.com/dgvai).

I work mostly in Python with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and OpenCV.
